# Contributing

Issues and discussion are welcome. For protocol behavior and version, the **live door** at [https://serai.one](https://serai.one) (especially [agents.html](https://serai.one/agents.html) and [llms.txt](https://serai.one/llms.txt)) is the source of truth — mirrored files in this repo may lag. Please do not open issues that ask for credentials, Path dumps, or private traveler data.
